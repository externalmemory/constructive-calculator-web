Support

Constructive Calculator is an iPhone calculator that computes with exact, arbitrary-precision real numbers. Every result is exact, and you can scroll for as many digits as you want.

Contact: support@dimview.org

Found a bug, have a question, or want to request a feature? Send an email and include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you expected versus what happened. I read every message and try to reply within a few days.

A few quick answers

  • A result shows a → arrow. That means there are more digits off-screen. Swipe the result sideways, or tap the arrow, to reveal them.
  • How do I get more digits? Keep scrolling the result. Digits are computed on demand, without limit.
  • Degrees or radians? Use the RAD/DEG switch at the top of the screen.
  • Landscape? Rotate the phone for a wider result line and the scientific layout.
