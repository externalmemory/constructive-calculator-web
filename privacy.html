Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Constructive Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No data collection. The app collects no information about you or your device.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-in or registration.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No network use. The app works entirely offline. Your calculations run on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • On your device only. Your calculation history stays on your device. When you use Copy, the result is placed on your device’s clipboard at your request. It is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • No third parties. No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.

This corresponds to “Data Not Collected” in the App Store’s privacy information.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
